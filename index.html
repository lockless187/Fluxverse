Frequently asked questions

Where do games come from?

Games appear after a verified developer submits one and Cinder approves it. Sample or unlicensed games are not shown.

Can I buy games?

Paid checkout has not launched. Free games can be claimed if an approved game includes a supported download.

Can I change my username?

Yes. Open My Profile and save your new username. It must be available.

How do I block someone?

Open a player's profile, select Block, or find them in your Friends list. Blocked players cannot message you through the Cinder system.

How do I report inappropriate content?

Use Report next to a community post, comment, message or player profile. Reports are private and go to the admin review queue.

Send feedback

Tell us about bugs, suggestions, or account issues.

Feedback is stored privately for Cinder administrators. This is not a live chat or guaranteed response service.

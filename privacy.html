At a glance: We use account, social, and technical information to operate Cinder. Public posts can be seen by others; private support requests are handled separately. You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data.

Who operates Cinder

Cinder is an online gaming community and game-discovery website. The service is operated independently by an individual located in Saskatchewan, Canada, rather than by a registered or incorporated company. The operator is responsible for the handling of personal information. Contact the operator regarding privacy at Lockless.187@gmail.com.

This policy describes how we handle personal information when you use Cinder at fluxverse.lockless187.workers.dev.

Information we collect

  • Account information: login email address, username, display name, and authentication/account status. Passwords are processed by our authentication provider, Supabase; we do not need you to send us your password.
  • Profile and social activity: bio, avatar or profile image, profile appearance preferences, favorite games or status messages, public-profile choices, friend requests, friendships, blocks, messages, posts, comments, likes, bookmarks, game reviews, reports, and notifications.
  • Game activity: Wishlists, free-game Library claims, game achievement unlocks, profile badges, account anniversary milestones, and requested download access to games made available by approved developers.
  • Support and recovery: feedback, abuse reports, account-recovery requests, contact addresses supplied for support, tracking conversations, and submitted recovery information.
  • Technical data: our hosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses, browser and device information, timestamps, and request logs needed to deliver and secure the service. Cinder also uses browser storage/cookies for sign-in and site functionality.

Do not include passwords, identity documents, financial details, or private recovery codes in public posts or ordinary support messages.

Why we use it

We use information to create and secure accounts; show user profiles where allowed by privacy settings; run Friends, Community, messages, notifications, Library, and Wishlist; review reports and abuse; handle account recovery and support; and operate, troubleshoot, and protect the website.

For abuse prevention, we keep limited action counts, warning and moderation records, and temporary posting restrictions. Administrators use these records to review reports, prevent spam, and investigate misuse; automated limits can occasionally affect legitimate activity. Players can contact support to question a restriction.

We do not currently offer paid checkout or recurring subscriptions on Cinder. We would update this policy before introducing payment-related processing or materially different uses of information.

What other players can see

Other players may see public usernames, display names, avatars, biographies, and content shared publicly, such as community posts, comments, and reviews. Some profile information depends on the privacy controls available in your account settings.

Friend lists, messages, and support requests are designed for limited access. Please remember that recipients can copy or screenshot information you send, and that content reported for safety may be reviewed by authorized staff.

Service providers and transfers

Cinder currently relies on Supabase for authentication, database, and some file-storage functions; Cloudflare for website hosting and delivery; and external providers for web fonts and JavaScript libraries (including Google Fonts and jsDelivr). These providers may process technical or account data necessary to deliver the service.

Depending on provider configuration, some information may be stored or processed outside your country. We may also disclose information where required by law, to investigate security incidents, or to protect users and the service, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Security and recovery

We use account authentication, access controls, and other reasonable measures intended to protect personal information. No online platform can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password and recovery tracking codes secret.

Password reset emails are handled through our authentication provider. Forgot Email recovery requests create support tickets: a ticket code lets the holder see the ticket conversation; that code alone does not prove ownership of the claimed account. Staff must verify account ownership separately before assisting with sensitive changes.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and activity records while needed to provide the service and for legitimate support, security, or legal purposes. Retention periods vary by type of record. Some information can remain in backups or required records for a limited additional period. We will evaluate deletion requests subject to identity verification and applicable requirements.

Instant self-service deletion is not currently available. Signed-in players can download a limited JSON data snapshot or submit a verified deletion request from My Profile. We review deletion requests and perform actual account removal through a separate secure administrator process. To request full account-data access, correction, or deletion, contact Lockless.187@gmail.com, or use Help & Support if you can sign in. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

Your controls and choices

You can edit supported profile details, manage friendships and blocks, and change available privacy settings from My Profile. You may ask to access or correct your personal information and may request deletion where applicable. Some content you publicly shared may already have been seen by others.

Essential sign-in and security storage is needed to run the website. If optional analytics or advertising technologies are introduced later, this policy and any required choices or consent mechanisms will be updated first.

Children and age restrictions

Cinder is not designed for children under 13. If you are under the applicable age of majority, obtain a parent or guardian's permission where required. If we discover that we have collected information from a child where doing so was not permitted, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Age-assurance and parental controls are not currently implemented; they require further work before a child-directed launch.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Cinder changes. The date at the top identifies this draft's preparation date. Material changes should be communicated to users as required by law, and the updated policy will be posted here.
